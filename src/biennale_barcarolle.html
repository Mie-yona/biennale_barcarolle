Imagine...

You just arrived in Venice. The air carries salt and centuries of history. You have come to visit the highly acclaimed art exhibition "La Biennale di Venezia", whose reputation precedes it across the globe. Fantastic tales about breathtaking masterpieces swirl in your mind and now you can barely contain your excitement.

In the spirit of Venice you board one of the famous gondolas waiting at the docks of the Grand Canal. And you start your journey. As the boat glides forward, the gondolier begins to hum a melody unlike any other. At first, it seems merely incidental. But soon, you realise: this is no random tune. Each note carries meaning. This is a poem composed not in syllables, but in sound! Written in the world's most universal language: music!

It is your personal invitation!

Solresol

... is a constructed language that translates words into tunes. Created by the French music teacher and composer Jean-François Sudre in the 19th century, Solresol allows you to compose roughly 2800 distinct words using only seven notes.

Once proclaimed the "Universal Language" and the "Universal Musical Language", Solresol offers a dream: communication that transcends borders, cultures, and even spoken tongues.

Barcarolle

A barcarolle is a piece of classical music. It is sung by Venetian gondoliers and the rhythm is supposed to imitate the swaying waves.
